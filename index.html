About

Public health leadership grounded in implementation

I am a public health and global health professional with extensive experience in district health systems, malaria and vector control, disease surveillance, and community health. My work has connected technical public health practice with the realities of delivering services in resource-constrained settings.

Originally trained and professionally established in Uganda, I am now based in Los Angeles, California, bringing an implementation-focused perspective to U.S. and international collaborations.

What guides my work

Practical solutions. Meaningful partnerships. Respect for communities. Evidence-informed implementation. A commitment to turning ambitious health goals into services that reach people.
